Where it fails · and we say so

June 2023, replayed with a model trained only on the past

Reality: 134. Our forecast: 17. Right decision, wrong magnitude.

Nothing on the ground sees the first smoke day coming — not our model, not the baseline. AirCast is for the ordinary 1-in-9 bad day. Extreme smoke needs satellite data. We measured that gap instead of hiding it.

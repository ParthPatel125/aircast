# AirCast reel — optional voiceover guide

The film is `demo/AirCast.mov` (72 s, 1080p60, music included). It works
silent: every line of the story is on screen. If you want voices on it, drop
the .mov into iMovie or CapCut, lower the music track a little, record these
lines, and export. Suggested split: Parth 1 to 4, Anay 5 to 7.

**1. Hook (0:00 to 0:12)**
> This is the air in Chicago. On June 26th, 2023, it read nine micrograms.
> The next morning: one thirty-four. Some of the worst air on Earth.

**2. The decision (0:12 to 0:20)**
> We built AirCast for one person: a school athletics director deciding today
> whether tomorrow's practice is outside. One day in nine here is over the
> WHO guideline.

**3. How it works (0:20 to 0:32)**
> It predicts tomorrow's PM2.5 as the EPA monitors will measure it.
> Twenty-five public signals, including our favorite: air quality right now
> in four cities upwind. Minneapolis air today is Chicago air tomorrow.

**4. Live demo (0:32 to 0:44)**
> Here it is live. One command, a few seconds: tomorrow's number, a likely
> range, and a clear answer.

**5. Results (0:44 to 0:56)**
> On 315 days of 2025 the model never saw, it beats the simple guess by ten
> percent, twenty-five at three days out. Our watch alert caught 88 percent
> of bad days; warnings were right 77 percent of the time.

**6. Honesty (0:56 to 1:04)**
> And where it fails: nothing on the ground sees the first smoke day coming.
> Reality was 134, we said 17. Extreme events need satellites, and we
> measured that gap instead of hiding it.

**7. Close (1:04 to 1:12)**
> AirCast. Honest forecasts for the days that can be forecast.
